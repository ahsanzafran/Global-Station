Software & Web Engineering

How long does it take to build a custom app or website?

A standard corporate website takes about 1–2 weeks. However, complex custom web platforms (Node.js/React) or mobile apps (Flutter) typically take 4–8 weeks depending on the features and backend requirements. We'll give you a clear timeline during the discovery phase.

Do you use templates or write custom code?

As hardcore software engineers, we write custom, scalable code from scratch. We don't rely on cheap templates. Every solution is architected to ensure maximum speed, enterprise‑grade security, and long‑term maintainability.

Do you provide ongoing technical support?

Yes! We offer dedicated maintenance packages. After launching your application, we continuously monitor servers, fix bugs, and push updates to keep your technology running flawlessly 24/7.

360° Digital Marketing & Growth

How long does it take to see results from SEO?

Because our SEO is deeply technical, our clients usually see a rank boost within the first 60–90 days. For instant traffic and immediate leads, we recommend combining SEO with our targeted PPC (Google/Meta Ads) campaigns.

Do you manage our social media completely?

Absolutely. Our 360° digital marketing retainer includes complete social media management. From designing creatives and writing copy to scheduling posts and analyzing audience data — we handle it all.
