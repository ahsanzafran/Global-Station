Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

At Global Station, accessible from www.globalstation.org, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website or submit project inquiries through our contact forms, we may collect:

  • Personal identification details (Name, Work Email, Phone Number, Company Name).
  • Project requirements, scope, and budget preferences.
  • Technical analytics data collected via Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity (IP addresses, browser type, device info, and page interaction).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly to respond to your inquiries, deliver custom software and digital marketing solutions, improve our website functionality through analytics insights, and maintain secure communication. We never sell or share your data with third-party advertisers.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We utilize tracking tools like Google Analytics (GA4) and Microsoft Clarity to analyze user navigation patterns and enhance visitor experience. These tools use cookies to collect standard internet log information anonymously.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach us at Marketing@globalstation.org or visit our office at Office 3-5, First Floor, Murat Khan Centre, 4 Montgomery Road, Lahore.
